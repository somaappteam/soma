# Soma Pro: Features That Feel Worth Paying For

This roadmap focuses on **high-perceived-value Pro features** that are hard to copy and align with Soma's existing social + learning experience.

## Positioning (what Pro means)

Pro should feel like: **"serious progress + elite social play."**

- **Free** = explore and learn casually.
- **Plus** = socialize and play without limits.
- **Pro** = optimize outcomes, status, and competitive edge.

## 1) Personal Coach & Advanced Learning Intelligence

These are the strongest long-term retention features.

### 1.1 Weakness Heatmap + Recovery Plans
- Show each learner's weak topics by error rate, recency, and forgetting risk.
- Offer one-tap plans like:
  - "Rescue my weak vocabulary"
  - "7-minute pronunciation recovery"
  - "Before-bed retention boost"

**Why it feels Pro:** users get a clear, personalized reason to open the app daily.

### 1.2 Predictive Streak Protection
- Detect when users are likely to miss daily goals.
- Push a short adaptive drill before streak break.
- Add a Pro-only "smart streak shield" (limited auto-protection events/month).

**Why it feels Pro:** turns anxiety into confidence with premium automation.

### 1.3 Mastery Forecast
- ETA to mastery by skill/topic.
- "At your current pace: Topic X mastery in 9 days."
- Weekly progress delta vs prior week.

**Why it feels Pro:** makes progress measurable and concrete.

## 2) Social Prestige & Competitive Advantages

Premium users want visible status and better competition flow.

### 2.1 Pro Identity Layer
- Pro badge + animated profile frame.
- Premium card effects in circles/lobbies.
- Distinct join/entry animation in circles.

### 2.2 Pro Ranked Seasons
- Seasonal ranked ladders with division tiers.
- Pro-only seasonal rewards (frames, emotes, titles).
- "Top Performer" spotlight slots.

### 2.3 Tournament Access
- Weekly Pro tournaments with curated rule sets.
- Bracket history, best finish, and season recap.
- Optional team/circle-based tournaments.

**Why it feels Pro:** public recognition drives social status and retention.

## 3) Voice & Real-time Premium Experience

You already identified this direction; make it concrete and measurable.

### 3.1 Priority Voice Routing
- Assign Pro users to lower-latency voice paths when possible.
- Fast reconnect and resilient fallback behavior.

### 3.2 Enhanced Voice Quality Profiles
- Pro audio bitrate profile options.
- Noise suppression and voice clarity tuning.
- "Connection quality" indicator in-call.

### 3.3 Pro Voice Controls
- Per-room mic profiles (quiet room / noisy room / mobile).
- Push-to-talk + quick mute presets.

**Why it feels Pro:** users hear and feel the difference immediately.

## 4) Creation & Hosting Power Tools

Give Pro users tools that make them community builders.

### 4.1 Advanced Private Circle Controls
- Saved room templates (mode, duration, question mix).
- Co-host controls and moderation queue.
- Fine-grained invite permissions.

### 4.2 Pro Event Builder
- Schedule recurring events.
- Auto reminders + RSVP management.
- Event analytics (attendance, completion, replay).

### 4.3 Premium Matchmaking Controls
- Priority queue placement.
- Match preferences (difficulty, language, mode bias).

**Why it feels Pro:** creators and hosts become power users.

## 5) Messaging Features Worth Paying For

Based on your plan: free text, premium media/voice behavior.

### 5.1 Pro DM Suite
- Larger per-file upload size than Plus.
- Rich media bundles (multiple files + captions).
- High-quality voice notes and transcription search.

### 5.2 Smart Attachments
- Auto compression presets by network quality.
- "Best quality" toggle for Pro.
- Attachment send diagnostics and retry UI.

### 5.3 Safety + Control
- Optional disappearing media windows.
- Advanced DM privacy controls and delivery confirmations.

## 6) Professional Analytics (for learners and creators)

### 6.1 Pro Progress Dashboard
- Learning velocity, retention decay curves, strongest/weakest domains.
- Study-time ROI by mode (solo vs circle vs review).

### 6.2 Circle/Host Analytics
- Session completion rates.
- Participant return rate.
- Voice quality and drop-off trends.

**Why it feels Pro:** transforms the app into a "serious tool," not just a game.

## 7) Feature Bundles by Tier (Clean Separation)

### Free
- Core solo learning.
- Public circles with limits.
- Basic DM text.

### Plus
- Social freedom bundle (no ads, unlimited sessions, unlimited media count with size caps, unlimited voice calls).

### Pro
- Everything in Plus **plus**:
  - Coaching intelligence
  - Ranked/tournaments
  - Priority voice quality
  - Pro identity/status
  - Hosting/event tools
  - Advanced analytics

## 8) Fast Rollout Sequence (4 Weeks)

### Week 1: Visible Pro Value
- Pro badge/profile effects.
- Pro events banner + tournament entry shell.
- Voice quality indicator UI.

### Week 2: Competitive Value
- Ranked season MVP.
- Pro queue priority logic.
- First Pro-only event.

### Week 3: Learning Intelligence MVP
- Weakness heatmap.
- Daily recovery plan generation.

### Week 4: Analytics & Retention
- Pro progress dashboard.
- Streak risk prediction + rescue drill CTA.

## 9) Success Metrics to Track

- Pro trial start rate.
- Trial-to-paid conversion.
- Pro 30-day retention vs Plus.
- Avg sessions/week for Pro.
- Tournament participation and repeat rate.
- Voice call quality satisfaction (post-session thumbs up/down).

## 10) Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Do **not** lock all core learning behind Pro.
- Do **not** make Pro only cosmetic.
- Do **not** ship Pro promises without reliability (voice/connectivity).
- Do **not** create unclear overlap between Plus and Pro.
