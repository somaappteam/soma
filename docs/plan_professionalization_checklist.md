# Making Free / Plus / Pro feel Professional

This checklist helps turn the existing 3-tier plan structure into a **clear, trustworthy, conversion-friendly pricing system**.

## 1) Fix positioning and naming clarity

A professional plan system starts with crystal-clear value boundaries:

- **Free (Starter):** try core learning and social basics.
- **Plus (Most Popular):** remove friction and unlock full social usage.
- **Pro (Power Users):** serious outcomes, status, and premium performance.

### Rules
- Keep **one headline benefit** per tier.
- Avoid repeating the same feature with slightly different wording.
- Make Plus and Pro visibly different in purpose, not just quantity.

## 2) Use measurable limits (avoid vague words)

Replace words like "limited" with exact numbers and units.

Examples:
- "3 circle sessions/day"
- "10 minutes/session"
- "10 MB per file (Plus), 100 MB per file (Pro)"
- "Unlimited files/day"

Professional plans always answer:
- **How much?**
- **How often?**
- **What quality level?**

## 3) Strengthen Plus as the default paid plan

Plus should solve daily pain points immediately:
- No ads
- Unlimited social sessions
- Unlimited voice calls
- Unlimited media sends (with file-size cap)

### Plus presentation tips
- Mark Plus as **Most Popular**.
- Show monthly + annual with savings badge (e.g., "Save 20% yearly").
- Keep pricing simple and psychologically clean.

## 4) Make Pro clearly premium (not just “Plus + extras”)

Professional Pro tiers include outcomes and status:

- Priority connection and improved voice quality.
- Ranked seasons and Pro-only tournaments/events.
- Pro badge/profile effects.
- Advanced analytics and learning coach insights.
- Early access to experimental modes.

If Pro does not improve performance or results, users see it as cosmetic and churn.

## 5) Add trust components around pricing

Professional subscriptions require explicit trust UX:

- Billing cadence: monthly/annual labels.
- Cancellation policy in plain language.
- Trial terms and renewal rules.
- "Manage subscription" entry point in settings.
- Restore purchases and receipts history.

## 6) Improve paywall and settings copy quality

Use product-grade wording:

- Avoid all-caps marketing claims.
- Keep sentence case and action-oriented CTAs.
- Use consistent terms across screens (e.g., "Circle sessions" everywhere).

### Good CTA examples
- "Upgrade to Plus"
- "Start Pro trial"
- "Compare plans"
- "Manage plan"

## 7) Monetization events and analytics (must-have)

Track the funnel professionally:

- Paywall view rate
- Plan card tap rate
- Trial start rate
- Trial-to-paid conversion
- 30-day retention by tier
- Churn reason capture at cancellation

Without this, you cannot optimize pricing quality.

## 8) Recommend feature matrix (clean final version)

## Free
- Unlimited solo learning
- Public circles
- 3 circle sessions/day (10 min each)
- DM text messages
- Voice/media with daily limits
- Ads in solo quiz results

## Plus
- Everything in Free, plus:
- No ads
- Unlimited circle sessions
- Private circles + friend invites
- Unlimited DM voice + media count
- File-size cap per attachment

## Pro
- Everything in Plus, plus:
- Higher file-size cap
- Priority connection + higher voice quality
- Pro badge/profile effects
- Premium matchmaking priority
- Pro events/tournaments
- Advanced progress analytics
- Early access game modes

## 9) Rollout checklist (execution guardrails)

Before shipping a new pricing model, confirm:

- Product copy is consistent across paywall, settings, and billing screens.
- Pricing experiments have a clear success metric and end date.
- Support FAQ is updated for trial, billing, and cancellation questions.
- Instrumentation is verified in production analytics dashboards.
- Plan entitlements are tested end-to-end for Free, Plus, and Pro users.

## 10) Rollout order to look professional quickly

1. **Copy/limits pass** (exact limits + consistent wording)
2. **Billing trust pass** (trial terms, manage/restore UI)
3. **Pro differentiation pass** (voice quality + events + analytics)
4. **Data pass** (analytics + conversion dashboards)
